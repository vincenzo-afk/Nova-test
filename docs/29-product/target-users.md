# Target Users

Primary: technically comfortable individuals running significant parts of their work and personal life across many apps/devices who want continuity without manually re-explaining context. Secondary: small-team power users who want a shared automation layer without building custom scripts per person. Explicitly not targeted at this stage: fully non-technical users needing zero-configuration onboarding, or enterprise compliance-heavy deployments (see `docs/00-overview/non-goals.md`).
