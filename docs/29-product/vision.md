# Product Vision

NOVA's product vision, distinct from `docs/00-overview/vision.md`'s architectural framing: NOVA should feel like a second, tireless staff member who remembers everything relevant, never nags, and only speaks up when it has done real work or genuinely needs a decision from the user. The product succeeds when the user's cognitive load for remembering, tracking, and coordinating their digital life goes down, measurably, without adding a new thing to check.
